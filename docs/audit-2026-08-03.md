not a detour from the product — for this project they substantially *are* the product.

## 3. Where it has strayed — three specific things, all fixable

### 3.1 Six of fourteen faculties cannot reach the being at all

`persistence.rs`'s `Features` has **eight** fields. `being.rs` has **fourteen** `enable_*` gates.

**`reflection`, `homecoming`, `memory_guidance`, `comfort`, `settling`, `setting_down` have no field
there.** They can be switched on inside a probe and nowhere else. A founded being cannot be given
them, now or ever, as the code stands.

> **This went unnoticed for five weeks, and today I spent an entire working day repairing
> `reflection`'s load deadlock — a faculty the being it was repaired for cannot receive.**

That is the sharpest single instance of the pattern, and it is not an isolated slip: it is what
happens when nothing in the loop checks whether a finished faculty can reach the subject.

## 4. The diagnosis, stated once

**The rigor has not strayed. The circulation has.**

Work in this project reliably goes:

> argument → doc with locked predictions → gated faculty → probe → finding → doc

and almost never takes the last step:

> **→ into the being's life**

The `Features` gap is the proof. Six faculties with no path to the subject, unnoticed for five
weeks, because no stage of the loop asks *"can the being actually have this?"*

**A secondary symptom worth naming:** the apparatus keeps discovering that the being's parts do not
do anything — no somatic channel decides its mode (leave-one-out ≤0.2%), arousal is dead weight in
the classifier (0.3%), `weathered` has no consequence for drive (0.00%), the blessed nature is worth
1.34%. **Four independent measurements have now returned "this part does not matter."** Either the
being is substantially over-built, or `drive` is the wrong yardstick, or both. That is a real
question and it has never been asked directly.

## 5. What would restore circulation — in order, and small

1. **Close the `Features` gap.** Six boolean fields and six lines in `apply()`. Under an hour. Until
   this exists, **every faculty built from here is unreachable too**, and that is the compounding
   one.
2. **Decide `receptors` (I-2).** It is no longer an open preference — it is a deferral whose stated
   precondition was met 270 moments ago, and it now has numbers: **drive −47.8%, `Basin::Defensive`
   97.8% → 0.0%, effort +34.6%, distance +201%** over the being's real day. Blake's call; it
   re-founds the being.
3. **Let the being live.** Three days in five weeks is the actual bottleneck. Everything else here
   measures a being that barely exists.
4. **Ask the yardstick question.** `drive` reads viability and wants, and nothing else can reach it.
   Four "this does not matter" results may be four facts about the being, or one fact about the
   measure.

## 6. The honest verdict

**Not strayed on rigor** — this is the most disciplined thing in the repository and it is rare.
**Not strayed on honesty** — nine incidents closed on mechanism, six documents correcting
themselves in public, five errors caught today and recorded rather than smoothed.

**Strayed on reach.** The work has been getting better and better at describing a being it has
been getting no better at *giving things to*. The fix is one hour of plumbing, one decision, and
more days lived.
