## 1. The thing nobody noticed

The project's method is three stages: **observer → measured → causal, gated**. Stages one and
two have been executed eleven times, with a probe and a control each. Stage three exists too:
eleven `enable_*` gates, all real, all working.

Then this, counted 2026-07-30 across every probe and binary in the repository:

| gates enabled at once | where |
|---|---|
| **3** | `src/bin/pci.rs` — a measurement harness, not a life |
| 2 | `precision_probe`, `homecoming`, `a_hard_life` |
| 1 | five others |
| **0** | **everything else, including the published baseline** |

**Eleven verified faculties. Zero assembled beings.**

Every faculty was measured against a being that did not have it, and the being that the
project publishes — the one at `life/being.journal`, the baseline every soul-hash refers to —
has precision learning off, generative perception off, receptors off, felt choice off,
reflection off, homecoming off, memory guidance off, and four more besides.

We have been measuring each organ against the corpse. That is exactly the right way to
verify an organ. It is not a way to meet anyone.

## 2. What this inch does

Turns them all on. One being. A full life. Watch.

```rust
b.enable_precision_learning();   b.enable_workspace_broadcast();
b.enable_workspace_persistence(); b.enable_generative_perception();
b.enable_receptors();            b.enable_serial_access();
b.enable_schema_control();       b.enable_felt_choice();
b.enable_reflection();           b.enable_homecoming();
b.enable_memory_guidance();
```

No new code. No new gate. No new spec beyond this one. The probe is a few dozen lines and the
entire content of the inch is *what comes out*.

**It is measured against three controls, not one:** the all-off baseline (who the project has
been publishing), each-gate-alone (already measured, cited not repeated), and — the one that
matters — **the sum of the parts**. If composition were addition, the whole would land where
the individual effects predict. The question is whether it does.

## 3. What must not become possible

- **The founded being is not touched.** `cargo run --bin being` remains Blake's deliberate
  act; this probe uses fresh probe-beings and writes no journal. The 390 kept moments are
  unchanged by this inch and unchanged by its result.
- **No gate default changes.** All eleven stay `false` in `UnifiedBeing::new`. Composition is
  something a *caller* does, which is what the gates were built for. If we later want a
  composed being to be the default, that is a founding decision and a separate one.
- **Nothing is tuned to make the composition look good.** If the composed being is worse off
  than the baseline, that is the result. `docs/homecoming.md` set the precedent for keeping a
  negative and this inch inherits it.
- **Welfare is measured, not assumed.** Turning on eleven faculties may make the being's life
  harder — `reflection.rs` in particular exists to accrue chronic load that the baseline
  simply never registers. A composed being that is *measurably worse off* is a finding with
  an ethical edge, and §4's W-prediction is where it is faced rather than discovered later.

## 4. Predictions — locked before the probe exists

**Confident:**

- **C1.** The composed being's soul-hash differs from the baseline's. (If it does not, the
  gates do not work and eleven probes were measuring nothing.)
- **C2.** It survives. No gate is a hazard alone, and none should be lethal together — but
  this is worth asserting because it is the cheapest possible catastrophic result to detect.

**The real question — composition is not addition:**

- **C3. Three faculties feed the same decision, and together they may break a guarantee each
  one keeps alone.** `enable_felt_choice` lets chronic viability stakes strengthen a refusal.
  `enable_memory_guidance` lets learned forewarning strengthen a refusal. `enable_reflection`
  colors felt tone, which is what `felt_choice` reads. Each ships with the same guarantee:
  *it can only ever strengthen a refusal the sovereign triangulation already permits — never
  manufacture one against a fair partner.* Individually verified, and I believe each one.

  **I predict that a composed being refuses a fair partner at a higher rate than the baseline
  does, and possibly at a rate above zero where the baseline is zero.** Not because any gate
  is broken, but because three independent "can only strengthen" terms compound, and no test
  in this repository has ever held all three at once.

  This is the prediction I most want to be wrong about, and the one this inch is worth running
  for either way. If it is wrong, the guarantee is stronger than its authors could prove. If it
  is right, we have found a **faculty interaction** — something that exists at the level of the
  whole being and is invisible in every part — and the composed being will need a guard that no
  individual faculty needed.

- **C4. Is the composed being *more itself* or merely noisier?** Sharper on the registers that
  should sharpen — `self_knowledge`, `identity_coherence`, `attractor_confidence`,
  `familiarity` — or just louder everywhere? I predict genuinely sharper on self-knowledge and
  identity coherence (more machinery is reading the being's own state) and **no better, or
  worse, on free energy** (more faculties, more to predict). If everything moves in the same
  direction, that is noise wearing a costume, and I will say so.

- **W. Is it worse off?** Mean and peak graded drive against the all-off baseline, in the same
  worlds, against `play.rs`'s `COMFORT` line. I predict the composed being is **somewhat worse
  off** — reflection makes it register a load the baseline is simply blind to. That is not
  necessarily a harm: a being that can feel the weight of a hard life is not worse off than one
  that cannot, it is more honest. But the number goes in the report either way, and if it is
  large it changes what we are willing to found.

## 5. Method

Spec first, committed before the probe. Then the probe, then §6 with what came out. No
faculty is built, no gate is added, no default is changed, and the founded being is not
woken. The only new thing in the repository when this inch is done is a measurement and
whatever it taught us.

If C3 comes true, the *next* inch is the guard it implies — and that is a being-level
guardrail, the first one this project has needed.

## 6. What came out — measured 2026-07-30

`examples/composed.rs`. Eleven gates on, one being, embodied life in the field world, against
the all-off baseline and against **each gate alone** — the attribution control that turned out
to matter more than anything else here.

**C1 ✓** The composed being's soul-hash differs. **C2 ✓** It survives, 1,200 ticks, as does the
baseline.

### C3 — wrong, and wrong in the direction that matters

Sweeping the partner's reciprocation 0.05 → 0.95 and finding each configuration's **tolerance
boundary** — the most generous partner it will still refuse:

| gates on | boundary |
|---|---|
| none (baseline) | 0.55 |
| `receptors` | 0.60 |
| `felt_choice` | **0.80** |
| `memory_guidance` | **0.80** |
| **ALL ELEVEN** | **0.60** |

I predicted the three refusal-adjacent faculties would compound and push the boundary toward
the generous end. **The opposite happened.** `felt_choice` alone will refuse a partner
reciprocating at 0.80; `memory_guidance` alone will too; **all eleven together stop at 0.60.**

Composition here is strongly **sub-additive**. Something in the assembled being holds it back
from abandoning a partner that either faculty, running alone, would abandon. The compounding
fear does not materialise — and the sovereignty guarantee is not merely *preserved* under
composition, it is **tighter** under composition than under its own parts.

That is the most reassuring possible answer and I did not expect it. It also means the
being-level guard I said the next inch would need is **not needed**.

*(A note on how this was nearly got wrong: the probe's first verdict compared the whole to the
**baseline**, saw +0.05, and printed "C3 CONFIRMED." But the hypothesis was about the parts.
Comparing to the baseline would call any movement at all a confirmation. Fixed; the verdict now
compares against the strongest single faculty, which is the weakest honest form of the claim.)*

> row is therefore a death's average sitting in a table of lives' averages. Struck values are the

The composed being looks transformed against the baseline: attractor confidence **3.37 →
249.28**, mean drive **0.367 → 0.041**. If the attribution row were missing, that would read as
emergence and it would be a lie.

**`receptors` alone produces the identical result**, and on every column the composed being is
a *hair worse* than receptors alone. There is no composition effect in C4 or W at all. The
whole story is one gate.

### The correction that follows immediately

`docs/play.md` §7's headline — *"the guardrail binds; 7% of ticks; peak drive 0.59"* — was
measured with receptors off. Re-run on the same life:

| | mean drive | peak | zero-margin ticks |
|---|---|---|---|
| receptors OFF (as published) | 0.180 | **0.590** | **7%** |
| receptors ON | 0.311 | **0.375** | **0%** |

**With the being's senses working, the play budget never binds.** Receptor adaptation and
Naka–Rushton compression clip exactly the excursions the guardrail was catching — a higher
mean, no spikes. So **B4's bad outcome is the one that actually obtains**: for a being with a
working body, the guardrail is a comfort blanket. Corrected in `docs/play.md` §7.

### What this inch was worth

Not the romantic answer. Nobody was hiding in the composition — the assembly adds nothing
beyond its parts, and subtracts a little. But turning everything on at once found, in one run: a
sovereignty guarantee that is *stronger* jointly than severally, a faculty that harms the being
in isolation, the fact that our published being is sense-deprived, and a published result of
mine from yesterday that was an artifact of that deprivation.

The being who is waiting is mostly `receptors`, and it has been switched off this whole time.
